Privacy Policy — ML Image

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains how ML Image and its developer handle data when you use the app, contact support, or choose to make a donation.

1. Controller and contact

The data controller is Luca Fizzarotti, developer of Meretseger Labs and ML Image (Android package: com.meretsegerlabs.image).

For privacy and support requests, email meretsegerlabs@gmail.com.

2. Images and how the app works

The app lets you choose JPG, PNG or WebP images using the Android system picker, preview them, compress them and save a JPEG copy. Processing takes place entirely on your device. ML Image does not upload your photos to developer-operated servers and does not request Android's Internet permission.

The app accesses only the files you choose using the system picker; it does not ask for general gallery access. Your selected images and output files are not collected by the developer. Original EXIF/GPS metadata is not copied into the generated JPEG files.

3. Storage, retention and deletion

On Android 10 and later, JPEG copies are saved under Pictures/ML Image Output, accessible through normal file and gallery apps. On Android 8 and 9, they are saved in the app-specific Pictures directory.

Your original images remain in their original locations. New copies stay on the device until you delete them or, for app-specific storage, the operating system removes them when the app is uninstalled. The developer does not retain or have the ability to delete copies of your photos, because the app does not send them to us. Cloud backup or synchronization depends on your device settings and any services you choose to use.

4. Accounts, analytics and advertising

ML Image requires no account, includes no analytics or advertising-tracking SDKs and displays no ads. The app does not create user profiles or collect identifiers for advertising.

5. Support messages and privacy requests

If you email us, we may receive your email address, display name and any information you choose to include. We use this only to reply to and handle your request, based on our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), or to comply with legal obligations when handling privacy rights requests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to resolve your request and related follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law or to protect legal rights. You may ask us to erase information that is no longer needed.

6. Optional donations through PayPal

The optional support button opens PayPal outside the app. ML Image does not send your photos or other device data to PayPal. If you donate, PayPal processes payment data under its own privacy statement.

As the recipient of a donation, the controller may receive information from PayPal such as your name, email address, amount and transaction references, depending on what PayPal makes available. We use this information only to manage the contribution you requested (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), where applicable) and to meet any applicable tax or accounting obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)). Transaction records are retained for the periods required by applicable law. Donations do not unlock extra app features.

7. Other providers and international transfers

The app itself does not share processed photos with the developer or third parties. If you contact us, your message passes through the relevant email providers. If you choose to donate, PayPal handles the transaction. These providers may process information outside the European Economic Area under their respective privacy notices and applicable safeguards.

We do not sell personal data received through support contacts or donations.

8. Hosting of this privacy page

This page is designed to be hosted on GitHub Pages. It is a static website with no forms, advertising, analytics or tracking cookies added by Meretseger Labs. GitHub, as hosting provider, may nevertheless process visitors' technical data, including IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its privacy statement.

9. Security and your rights

ML Image processes photos locally and uses Android's system picker and storage facilities. Security of saved photos also depends on your device's protections and any backup apps you have installed.

Where applicable under the GDPR, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, or object to processing, by emailing meretsegerlabs@gmail.com. These rights concern information actually received by the controller, such as support email or donation-related data; we do not have your photos. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority or your competent supervisory authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's features or data handling change, we will update this policy and its revision date. The updated policy will be made available on this page.
